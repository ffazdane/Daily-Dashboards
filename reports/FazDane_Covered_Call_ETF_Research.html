02 / What stands out

Three markets, three outcomes

Nasdaq: income strategies split widely

FTQI returned 16.0% and QYLD 15.7%, ahead of QQQ’s 10.3%. GPIQ and QDTE also finished ahead, while JEPQ and QQQI lagged. Fund design and portfolio selection mattered; the covered-call label alone did not predict the result.

FTQI’s advantage over QQQ: +5.7 percentage points.

S&P 500: smaller differences at the center

XYLD led this group at 14.5%, followed by GPIX at 13.2%. SPYI’s 11.9% was close to SPY’s 12.0%, despite its much larger cash distributions. JEPI and FTHI finished below the broad-market reference.

XYLD’s advantage over SPY: +2.5 percentage points.

Small caps: IWMI edged IWM

IWMI’s 15.2% exceeded IWM’s 14.6% by 0.6 percentage points. RYLD returned 12.1%, trailing IWM by 2.5 points. RDTE remains outside the ranking because comparable month-end data were not verified.

Capital illustration: $10,000 grew to $11,600 in FTQI, $11,030 in QQQ, $11,200 in SPY, and $11,460 in IWM. Values assume reinvestment and exclude personal taxes and trading costs.

04 / Interpreting the research

Income and wealth are different decisions

A covered-call strategy exchanges some potential upside for option premium. That income can help in certain market paths, but the fund still bears equity losses. Premium is a cushion, not principal protection.

A cash distribution moves value out of the fund. It can come from dividends, option-related gains, realized gains, or return of capital. A large payout alone does not show whether the investor became wealthier.

These one-year results support a closer review of FTQI, QYLD, IWMI, XYLD, and GPIX. Before selecting a holding, examine longer returns, drawdowns, NAV trends, fees, tax treatment, and current bid–ask spreads.

05 / Scope & limitations

What this report does—and does not—measure

Common endpoint: October 31, 2026 month-end reporting, using the last available market close where applicable. This is trailing one year, not calendar-year 2026 performance.

Method: Market-price total returns, distributions reinvested, rounded to one decimal. Fund operating expenses are reflected in reported returns; personal taxes and investor trading costs are excluded.

Excluded: AIPI had an issuer-reported one-year market return of 24.08% through September 30, 2026, a different endpoint. RDTE’s comparable October figure was not verified. Neither is ranked.

Liquidity: This report carries forward the fund universe from the earlier liquidity screen; it does not establish that every fund has equally tight spreads or deep trading liquidity.
