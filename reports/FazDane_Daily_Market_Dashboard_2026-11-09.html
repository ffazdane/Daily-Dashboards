10 / Source & method ledger

Breadth = percent of the 11 sector ETFs advancing over five sessions. Volatility = 100 × (35 − VIX) / 23. Clamp each component to 0–100. Below 40 is defensive, 40–59 neutral, and 60 or higher constructive. These are model readings, not probabilities.
