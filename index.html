About the reports

Reports distinguish dated market observations from model-generated tactical interpretations. Unverified IV, gamma, positioning, and trade signals must not be treated as live market data. Historic snapshots are for research, not trading execution.

New archive entries are added only after a successful, verified publication. The existing ChatGPT briefing does not yet automatically upload to GitHub.
