Market Update — Executive Summary

Index futures closed the week near their highs, with /ES at 7,870 and /NQ at 31,178. Underneath, participation kept deteriorating. Across roughly 4,800 US stocks, only 26.3% trade above their 50-day and 38.9% above their 200-day moving average, both near the bottom of their one-year range. Two weeks ago those readings were 28.2% and 41.2%, so the divergence widened while price held up.

The options market is pricing this fragility. SKEW at 154 means deep out-of-the-money S&P puts are expensive, while VIX at 14.8 says everyday volatility is cheap. Institutions are insuring against a sharp drop rather than selling.

Price vs Internals — The Divergence

  • Large caps vs everything else: S&P 500 members beat the broad market on every timeframe (47.8% vs 38.9% above the 200-day). Leadership is concentrated in mega-caps, which is why /NQ keeps rising while the typical stock does not.
  • Short-term bounce, long-term damage: the 10-day reading jumped +14.5 points in 20 sessions, but the 50-, 100- and 200-day readings fell 8.5 to 11.5 points. That is a relief rally inside a weak regime, not repair.
  • Dow split: /YM fell 195 points while /NQ gained, and Transports ($DJT) slipped. Cyclical confirmation is missing.
  • 50/200 ratio at 0.68: fast breadth is lagging slow breadth. It is not yet a washout (below 0.50), so the historically strongest buy setup has not triggered.

History says high SKEW is not a timing signal on its own. Since 2014, extreme SKEW days were followed by slightly below-average 63-day returns (+2.4% vs a +3.3% baseline). The last two spikes at this level or above, August 2018 and February 2025, each preceded roughly a 20% S&P drawdown within four months, while the June 2021 record was followed by calm gains. The combination of narrow breadth and expensive tails is what deserves respect.

Summary & Actionable Conclusions

Narrow Tape

Only 26% of stocks hold their 50-day while futures sit near highs. Index strength rests on a small group of leaders.

Expensive Tails

SKEW 154 with VIX under 15: quiet surface, costly crash insurance. Fragility is being hedged, not sold.

Levels to Watch

50/200 ratio below 0.50 (washout) or above 1.50 (thrust); % above 200-day reclaiming 50%.

Do Not Chase

Long /ES and /NQ exposure is a mega-cap bet. Size lighter and keep stops tighter than normal.

Bias

Neutral to cautious. Trend intact on price, unconfirmed by participation; small caps remain the weak leg.

Hedge Plan

With VIX and VVIX low, volatility protection is relatively cheap; selling deep OTM puts pays well but carries exactly the risk being priced.
