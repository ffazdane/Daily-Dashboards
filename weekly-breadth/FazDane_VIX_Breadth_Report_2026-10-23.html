Market Update — Executive Summary

VIX has climbed in three straight check-ins: 14.84 on Oct 9, 16.46 on Oct 15 and 19.45 on Oct 23. The S&P 500 touched a five-week low and closed at 7,623.68. Nasdaq futures did the most damage, with /NQ down about 6.4% since Oct 9 (31,178 to 29,175), as AI-capex worries hit tech after Meta held capex guidance flat and Tesla cut its capex guidance by 20%.

The important shift is where fear is being priced. Two weeks ago investors were buying cheap far out-of-the-money puts while VIX sat near 15, so SKEW hit 160. Now that insurance is being cashed in for at-the-money volatility: VIX and VVIX rose while SKEW fell back to 151.8. Fear moved from the tails into the body of the distribution. That is what a real volatility pickup looks like, rather than just hedging demand.

Chart — Fear Migration, Oct 9 to Oct 23

Figure 1: VIX, VVIX and CBOE SKEW at the Oct 9, Oct 15 and Oct 23, 2026 closes, from the FazDane ThinkorSwim watchlist. SKEW peaking while VIX rises is the classic hand-off from cheap tail hedges to broad volatility buying.

Why VIX Is Holding Above 16

Realized volatility is only 9.8% over the past month, yet VIX prices 19.45. That +9.7-point premium is unusually wide: options are pricing a cluster of scheduled and unscheduled risks, not what the market has actually delivered.

Retail fear is high but professional positioning is still heavy. That combination usually means the market has not fully flushed. A true capitulation tends to show up as VIX/VIX3M inverting above 1.0. Historically that has been a better entry than a warning: after past inversions, SPY's median one-month return was +2.54% (positive 74% of the time) versus +1.58% for all days.

Summary & Actionable Conclusions

Vol Regime Shift

VIX up from 14.8 to 19.5 in two weeks and VVIX at 109.7. Volatility is being bought, not just hedged.

Fear Hand-Off

SKEW peaked at 160 on Oct 15 and fell to 151.8 as VIX rose: tail insurance converted into broad volatility demand.

Breadth Improving

Stocks above the 50-day rose to 33.4% while /NQ fell about 6%. The damage is concentrated in AI and mega-cap tech.

Event-Driven Premium

VIX 19.5 vs 9.8% realized. Options price FOMC, mega-cap earnings and oil risk; a vol crush is likely if those pass quietly.

Key Lines

VIX 20, VVIX 110 and VIX/VIX3M 1.0. A close through all three marks escalation; staying under keeps it a grind.

Positioning
