Weekly Summary

This was the market's worst week since June. The S&P 500 fell 2.7% and closed below its 30-week moving average, a long-term trend warning. The Nasdaq lost 3.4%, its fourth straight weekly decline, and the Nasdaq-100 entered correction territory, about 12% below its Oct 6 record going into Friday. Stocks rallied Wednesday after the midterm elections, then Thursday's selloff erased an estimated $2.5 trillion as fears of tighter AI data-center rules hit semiconductors: the SOX fell about 12% in one day. Friday brought a partial bounce, with the Nasdaq up 1.09%.

Volatility crossed into a new regime. VIX closed above 20 for the first time in this cycle, peaked at 28.18 Thursday and finished at 25.64. The VIX/VIX3M ratio inverted above 1.0 for the first time since April, the capitulation-style signal flagged in our last two reports. Yet underneath, breadth held: the share of US stocks above their 50-day rose for a fourth straight week to 39.0%, even as the indexes fell.

Breadth Read

  • Divergence is now extreme: 50-day breadth rose to 39.0% while /NQ fell another 5% on the week and is down about 13% since Oct 9. The composite breadth reading for all US stocks jumped from the 33rd to the 51st percentile (5-year), back to "middling."
  • The damage is in the Nasdaq-100: composite breadth for Nasdaq-100 members sits at the 33rd percentile and falling. Semiconductors and storage names took the hit: Micron about −22%, Marvell, Seagate and Western Digital each roughly −28%.
  • Long-term trend slipped: stocks above the 200-day fell back to 37.5%, and the S&P closed below its 30-week average. The repair is short-term only.
  • Leadership rotated again: energy, last month's winner, fell about 12% as Brent dropped 13% on Iran de-escalation hopes. Homebuilders rallied 7–8% Wednesday; staples, healthcare and financials hit records early in the week before pulling back.
  • Cyclicals cracked: Transports fell 2.7% on the week to 19,775, their lowest close in our five-week series.

Summary & Actionable Conclusions

Regime Shift

VIX 25.6, VVIX 122, curve inverted. The calm 15–20 regime is over; expect wide daily ranges until the curve normalizes.

Breadth Resilient

50-day breadth up a fourth week to 39% while /NQ fell 13% from Oct 9. This is a tech correction, not a broad liquidation.

Capitulation Signals

VIX/VIX3M inversion, Fear & Greed 22, AAII −14 and record options volume. Historically constructive over 1–3 months.

Trend Damaged

S&P below its 30-week average; Nasdaq-100 in correction; 200-day breadth back to 37.5%. Not a confirmed bottom yet.

Levels to Watch

VIX/VIX3M back under 1.0; VIX 28.18 Thursday high; SPX support 7,350 / 7,240, resistance 7,440 / 7,550; CPI Tuesday.

Trade Plan

Scale in rather than chase; favor equal-weight and breadth leaders over semis. Keep risk defined while VIX1D sits above VIX.
